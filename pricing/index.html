Why One-Time and Not a Subscription?

Pro is a one-time purchase. We run no servers that hold or read your documents. Your document files stay on your device unless you share or export them, or turn on encrypted backup to your own cloud (Pro). That's why we can offer a one-time price instead of endless subscriptions. Pro has no recurring subscription charges.

A one-time price is fair, sustainable, and honest. You pay once. We stop asking.

What's Included Free, Forever

The free tier isn't a trial. It's a real, permanent tier with core features you'll actually use: one profile, 5 documents, expiry reminders, document sharing, biometric lock, on-device encryption, and vault export and import. If you're solo or just testing, free is all you need.

No time limits. No features that mysteriously disappear. No dark patterns pushing you to upgrade.

What Pro Adds

Pro is for families. Unlimited profiles for your whole family, unlimited documents, trip planner with a visual readiness checklist, encrypted cloud backup to your own iCloud or Google Drive, and a days-abroad tracker for per-country day limits and tax residency tracking.

One purchase on your account. One family. Works on all your devices that use the same App Store or Google Play account. The App Store and Google Play each sell Pro separately, so moving between iPhone and Android means buying it once more.

One Purchase, Whole Family

When you buy Pro, you're not paying per person or per device. You create one vault with unlimited profiles - one for you, one for your partner, one for each child. Everyone's documents. One charge.

No subscriptions. No seat-based pricing. No per-family-member charges.

Pricing Questions

How much does Travel Document Vault cost?
Travel Document Vault offers a free tier forever - 1 profile with up to 5 documents, including all core features like expiry reminders, document sharing, and encryption. Pro is a one-time purchase and unlocks unlimited profiles, unlimited documents, encrypted cloud backup, trip planner, and days-abroad tracker.
Is Travel Document Vault a subscription?
No. Pro is a one-time purchase. You pay once for Pro and own it forever. No recurring charges, no subscription cancellation needed, and no trials that quietly start billing you. The free tier is also free forever.
What do I get in free versus Pro?
Free: 1 profile, up to 5 documents, expiry reminders, document sharing, PIN and biometric lock, on-device encryption, offline access. Pro adds: unlimited profiles for your family, unlimited documents, trip planner with family readiness traffic light, encrypted cloud backup to your own iCloud or Google Drive, days-abroad tracker for visa and tax limits, and custom reminder timing.
Do I pay per family member or per device?
No. Pro is a one-time purchase for your account. Once purchased, you can add unlimited family members (profiles) and use the app on multiple devices under the same App Store or Google Play account. One purchase. Whole family.
What does 'all v1.x updates included' mean?
Your purchase covers every update within the current major version (v1.x), including bug fixes, security patches, and new features. This applies for as long as you use the app. If we ever release v2.0 with substantial architectural changes, early adopters get preferential upgrade pricing and the option to stay on v1.x Pro forever. See our full version policy for details.
