Can You Travel While Your Passport Is Being Renewed?

Travel Document Vault

March 14, 2026 · 7 min read

Key Takeaways

  • Travel during renewal depends on your application route, where you apply, and whether your passport remains valid and in your possession.
  • United States: Mail-in renewal means sending your current passport in with the application. Domestic travel is fine. International travel requires your renewed passport to arrive first.
  • United Kingdom: Postal and standard online renewals generally require you to send your old passport to HM Passport Office. Applying digitally does not let you keep travelling on it. Wait until your new passport arrives before booking travel.
  • Australia and Canada: Requirements depend on where and how you apply. Some Australian overseas renewals allow continued use of a valid passport. Canada's paper renewal guidance for applicants outside Canada and the United States generally requires the still-valid original. Check with the relevant passport office.
  • If travel is urgent, ask your passport office which options you qualify for. International travel generally requires a valid passport or another travel document accepted for your route.

Travelling while a renewal is in progress may be possible if your passport remains valid and in your possession. The answer depends on your renewal method and where you apply. UK online renewal does not create an exception that lets you keep travelling on the old passport.

A Renewal in Progress Won't Get You Through Border Control

International travel generally requires a valid passport physically in your possession. A renewal application or acknowledgement letter does not replace it. Check the document requirements for your route, including whether an emergency travel document would be accepted if you qualify for one.

Whether you can use your current passport depends on your renewal method, where you apply and whether it remains valid.

United States: Mail-In Renewal

Standard US passport renewal by mail means sending your current passport in with the application, so it isn't in your hands while you wait. You complete the DS-82 form, enclose your existing passport, and post everything to the National Passport Processing Center. USAGov says your old passport is mailed back separately, usually about four weeks after you receive the new one.

This means domestic travel within the United States is unaffected throughout the renewal process - you don't need a passport for domestic flights. International travel is a different story: you can't travel internationally until your new passport arrives, and you can't use the old cancelled passport even if you have it in hand.

Standard processing times change, and the State Department publishes the current figure at travel.state.gov, so check there before you plan around it. If your travel is booked before you expect the new passport to arrive, expedited processing is available for an additional fee and cuts the wait meaningfully. For genuinely urgent travel, in-person appointments are available at regional passport agencies - check current eligibility at travel.state.gov.

United Kingdom: Send Your Old Passport to HMPO

UK postal and standard online renewals generally require you to send your old passport to HM Passport Office. Applying online does not let you keep it for travel throughout processing.

Postal renewal: You post your current passport to HM Passport Office along with your application. Once you send it, you cannot use that passport for international travel. HM Passport Office publishes its current processing time on its website and advises against booking travel until your new passport arrives. If your trip is close, check which urgent passport services you qualify for.

Online and Digital Check and Send renewals: Follow HM Passport Office's instructions for sending your old passport. With Digital Check and Send, the Post Office sends it to HMPO for you. You cannot travel on a passport you have sent away, and HMPO advises waiting until you receive your new passport before booking travel.

Australia: Check Where You Apply

Australian passport renewal arrangements depend on where you apply. The Australian Embassy in Germany says applicants there keep their current passport and can generally use it while it remains valid during processing. That overseas process does not establish the rule for applications within Australia. Check with the Australian Passport Office or the mission handling your application before planning travel.

Check current processing times and application instructions with the Australian Passport Office at passports.gov.au. If travel is urgent, ask the office handling your application which options apply to you and when your current passport will be cancelled.

Canada: Check Your Renewal Method

Canada's requirements depend on where and how you apply. Government guidance for paper renewals from outside Canada and the United States generally requires the original passport if it is still valid. You cannot travel on a passport you have submitted. For applications within Canada or other renewal methods, check the Government of Canada's instructions and confirm with the passport office whether you must submit your passport and when it becomes invalid.

Check current processing times at canada.ca for your application route. If you have urgent travel, ask the passport office which services you qualify for before relying on a particular turnaround.

Can You Travel on Your Old Passport While Renewing?

This is the question most people are really asking, and the answer is narrower than it looks. Holding your old passport isn't the same as being able to fly on it.

Two things have to be true at once. The passport has to still be in your possession, and it has to still be valid for your trip. Check both with the office handling your renewal.

UK online renewal does not let you keep travelling on the old passport throughout processing. You generally need to send it to HMPO, so plan around receiving your new passport.

US mail-in renewal is the case people most often get wrong. You post your existing passport in with the application, so you don't hold it during processing. USAGov says it is mailed back separately after your new passport is mailed and generally arrives about four weeks after you receive the new one. A cancelled passport won't get you through check-in, even though it's yours and it's in your hand.

For Australia and Canada, check the instructions for your application route and location. The Australian mission in Germany allows continued use while the current passport remains valid. Canada's paper renewal guidance for applicants outside Canada and the United States generally requires you to submit a still-valid passport.

A cancelled or expired passport is generally unsuitable for international travel, and you cannot use one that is sitting in a government office. For domestic flights, check which other identification your airline and the relevant transport authority accept.

If Your Trip Is Sooner Than Your Renewal Timeline

Don't wait to contact your country's passport service by phone - ask specifically about expedited, urgent, or emergency appointment options. These exist in every country listed here, but availability changes week to week and appointment slots disappear quickly.

At the same time, contact your airline about rescheduling. Some carriers will waive change fees when a genuine document issue is the reason. Get any agreement in writing. If you have travel insurance, check your policy for coverage of costs arising from passport delays or document problems.

Renewing Early Removes the Timing Pressure Entirely

The straightforward answer is to renew your passport well before you need it for travel. Applying 6 to 9 months before any planned international travel removes the timing pressure entirely and leaves room for standard processing without expedited fees. We'd apply nearer nine months out than six, so standard processing has room to run long without touching a booked trip. If you don't already know your own expiry date, check it today, before you book anything.

The reason people end up in this situation is not ignorance of the rules. Passports expire quietly with no automatic reminder, so people tend to remember only that their passport is valid, not when it expires. Setting a reminder 12 months before your expiry date gives you time to renew at your own pace, on standard processing, without pressure.

Keep your own passport and visa dates on your phone, with reminders before they expire.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I travel domestically while my passport renewal is in progress?

In most cases, yes. Domestic travel within your own country does not require a valid passport. You will typically need alternate government-issued ID such as a driver's licence. For domestic flights in the United States, a passport is not required at all.

Can I travel internationally while my passport is being renewed?

It depends on your renewal method, where you apply and whether your passport remains valid and in your possession. US mail-in renewal requires you to send it in, and UK postal and standard online renewals generally do too. Some Australian overseas renewals allow continued use of a valid passport. Check with the passport office handling your application before planning travel.

Can I travel on my old passport while renewing?

Generally, only if it remains valid for your trip and in your possession. UK online renewal does not let you keep travelling on the old passport throughout processing. US mail-in renewal requires sending it in, and a cancelled passport is not valid for travel. Australian and Canadian requirements depend on where and how you apply, so check the relevant passport office's instructions.

What happens if my flight is booked but my passport won't arrive in time?

Contact your passport service immediately and ask about expedited or urgent processing. Most countries offer faster options for a fee. If those timelines do not work, you may need to reschedule your flight or apply for an emergency travel document if your circumstances qualify.

Do I keep my passport during the renewal process?

It depends on where and how you apply. US mail-in renewal requires sending your old passport in; USAGov says it generally arrives back separately about four weeks after you receive the new one. UK postal and standard online renewals generally require sending it to HMPO. The Australian mission in Germany lets applicants keep it, while Canada's paper renewals from outside Canada and the United States generally require a still-valid original. Check your passport office's instructions.

What is an emergency travel document?

An emergency travel document or emergency passport may be available when you need to travel urgently. Eligibility, validity, permitted journeys and processing times depend on the issuing authority and your circumstances. Some countries may not accept it. Check with your passport authority and the authorities for your destination and transit countries before relying on one.
