Where Your Documents Probably Live Now

Most of us already keep copies somewhere. Here's how to get more out of each one.

Camera Roll / Photos

Always with you. Put passport photos in their own album so you're not scrolling past holiday snaps at the check-in desk.

Cloud Storage

Handy across devices. Give travel documents one clearly named folder per person, and turn on the strongest sign-in protection your provider offers.

Password Managers

A sensible home for a passport number if you already use one. Keep the photo page just as easy to reach for the airport.

Note-Taking Apps

Flexible and familiar. If a passport number lives in a note, lock that note where your app allows it.

Spreadsheets

Good for putting the whole family's expiry dates in one table. Add a "renew by" column and you'll see the next deadline at a glance.

Paper Copies

Still worth having. A printed copy of each photo page, packed separately from the passports, helps if one goes missing abroad.

Quick Answers

The questions we hear most often.

What happens if I lose my phone?
Your documents are encrypted on your device. The local key stays in secure storage and is excluded from ordinary phone backups. A vault export includes a password-encrypted copy of that key. A regular phone backup reinstalls the app but cannot restore your documents. Pro cloud backup restores with your recovery code on a supported phone on the same platform, using the same cloud account. Or use the free vault export and import. Restoring or importing replaces the local vault.
Does it work without internet?
Completely. The app stores everything on your device and needs no internet connection for everyday use. Reminders and locally saved document viewing work offline. OCR processing runs on your device.
Is my data really safe?
Your documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device, and the key is held in your phone's secure key storage, never included in a phone backup. There is no account to create and no Travel Document Vault server that holds your documents. If you turn on optional cloud backup (Pro), your vault is encrypted on your phone before upload and goes to your own iCloud or Google Drive, sealed with a recovery code only you hold. The design means I cannot read your data, and neither can Apple or Google - you can check the privacy model yourself on the Privacy Verification page. PIN lock and biometric lock (Face ID/Touch ID) are both free.
Is it really a one-time purchase?
Yes. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription. Each country's price is set locally, and the store shows yours before you pay.* No recurring charges and no "your trial is ending" emails. The free tier is genuinely free - 1 profile, 5 documents, all core features including OCR and reminders.

* For v1.x. See Pricing Policy.

Can I use it for more than passports?
Yes. It supports passports, visas, IDs, driving licences, insurance cards, airline tickets, and any document type you need. Each shape of document has its own guided capture overlay.
